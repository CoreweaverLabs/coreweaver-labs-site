Design Principles
01
Verification over trust
Every data payload, agent action, and system state must be independently verifiable. No trust assumptions — cryptographic proof or it didn't happen.
02
Incentive alignment
Agent behavior follows economic incentives. Systems are designed so correct behavior is the profitable behavior — aligned with Bittensor's miner/validator model.
03
Physical-digital continuity
The built environment and digital networks must converge. City infrastructure, industrial systems, and blockchain compute are one continuous layer — not separate domains.
04
Open by default
All Coreweaver Labs work is open-source unless a security constraint demands otherwise. The agentic web is built by people who share what they make.
